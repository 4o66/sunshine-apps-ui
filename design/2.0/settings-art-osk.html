Community artwork

SteamGridDB is a community library of game artwork, and the place to look when a game has no cover of its own — which is most often a GOG or Epic game, since Steam ships its own. It is optional, and everything else here works without it. With a key, the artwork picker grows a Show SteamGridDB art button — nothing is fetched from them until you press it.
